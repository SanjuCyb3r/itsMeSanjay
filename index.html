About Me

I am a cybersecurity engineer specializing in offensive security, digital forensics, and malware reverse engineering. My background includes serving as a Graduate Teaching Assistant at George Mason University, mentoring 250+ students in adversarial security practices, and conducting hands-on penetration testing during my internship at HackersForYou.

I bring a blend of red-team expertise, forensic analysis, and research-driven problem solving, grounded in frameworks like MITRE ATT&CK, NIST CSF, and Zero Trust. I’m focused on strengthening cyber-defense strategies while continuing to advance in ethical hacking and digital forensics.

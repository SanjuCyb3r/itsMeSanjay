About Me

I am a cybersecurity engineer and researcher specializing in offensive security, hardware side-channel analysis, and digital forensics. I work across tools including Nmap, Burp Suite, Metasploit, ChipWhisperer Husky, Wireshark, IDA Pro, and Splunk.

As a Security Research Assistant at Mason Innovation Lab, George Mason University, I implement VFD monitoring systems, data pipelines, and ML models under PI supervision. I also serve as a Student Teacher & Research Assistant (STAR), guiding students in cloud computing, virtualization on AWS and CloudLab, SSH/SCP protocols, and MapReduce.

My background further spans penetration testing at HackersForYou and mentoring 250+ students as a Graduate Teaching Assistant. Grounded in MITRE ATT&CK, NIST CSF, and Zero Trust, I connect red-team expertise with research-driven problem solving across hardware, cloud, and network security.
